Vendor voice assistant · controlled pilot

Human approval required
Confirm an already-approved order; never negotiate or commit funds.

Twilio connects the call. ElevenLabs voices the assistant. The operations backend supplies only the approved order, logs consent and sends the vendor a written recap.

Safe vendor call preview

This is the exact scope for the initial voice pilot. It explicitly asks for consent and cannot change a price, quantity or payment term.

“Hello, I’m HostelOps, an AI assistant calling for the hostel mess. Is it okay to continue with an automated call? I’m confirming approved purchase request MES-204: 50 kg tomatoes, delivery tomorrow between 6:30 and 7:00 AM. Can you confirm availability at the already approved terms? A staff member will review any changes.”

When the vendor mentions a change: create an exception ticket, notify the mess manager and send a written recap. No autonomous commitment.
